# For the Money

### NTR Adult Game Story Draft

---

## Main Characters

**Player (You)**  
Average guy, early-to-mid 20s. Not rich, not particularly confident. Loves his girlfriend but has been struggling with money for a while. Can be written as passive, jealous, enabling, or a mix depending on player choices.

**Jessy (Girlfriend)**  
23–25. Pretty, natural body, slightly shy but practical. Not a model at the start — just a normal girl with a part-time or unstable job. Loves you, but the constant money stress is wearing her down. She has a strong sense of “I have to do something” when things get bad. Once she starts modeling, the attention and money slowly change her.

**Leon (Main Bull / Photographer)**  
Early 30s. Tall, good-looking, calm, experienced. Runs a small but successful commercial photography studio. Knows exactly how to talk to insecure girls. Never forces anything openly at first — he creates situations where saying yes feels natural and saying no feels like she’s being difficult. Sees Jessy as both a good model and an easy conquest.

**Secondary characters (optional but useful)**

- Jessy’s friend who first suggests modeling
- A female assistant at the studio who normalizes the boundary-crossing
- A rich client who appears later for higher-paying “special” jobs

---

## Act 1 – Money Pressure (Chapters 1–4)

**Opening**  
You and Jessy live together in a small apartment. Rent is increasing, one of you lost hours at work, or there’s sudden debt (medical, family, credit card — player can choose the reason). Daily life is tense. Small arguments about spending. Sex still happens but feels more like stress relief than intimacy.

**Key events**

- Jessy starts looking for extra work. She tries normal part-time jobs but the pay is low.
- One day her friend (or a recruiter) tells her she has a good face and body for commercial modeling — product catalogs, online ads, lifestyle shoots. The pay is noticeably better.
- Jessy is hesitant. She says things like “I’m not pretty enough for that” or “It feels weird.”
- Player choices: encourage her, stay neutral, or try to talk her out of it.  
  Most paths still lead to her taking the first job because the money situation is bad.

**First shoot**  
She comes home after the first commercial shoot (clothes or cosmetics). She’s nervous but a little excited. The pay helps. She shows you some of the photos — clean, professional, nothing sexual. You feel a small mix of relief and unease.

**Tone of Act 1**  
Still a normal couple story. The NTR is only a distant possibility. The focus is on money stress and Jessy’s decision to try modeling.

---

## Act 2 – Getting Used to It (Chapters 5–10)

Jessy starts getting regular work. The money improves. The arguments at home decrease. She begins to enjoy the attention and the feeling of being useful.

**Introduction of Leon**  
On her third or fourth job she is assigned to Ren’s studio. He is polite, professional, and complimentary in a calm way. He tells her she has “good presence” and that he can get her better-paying jobs if she works with him more.

**Small boundary pushes**

- He adjusts her pose with his hands longer than necessary.
- Sessions run late.
- He asks her to stay a bit after the main shoot for “test shots” or portfolio work.
- He starts texting her about future jobs (player can see some of the messages if they check her phone).

**Jessy’s change**  
She talks about Leon more at home. She comes back tired but in a better mood. Sex with you becomes less frequent. When it happens, she seems distracted.

**First suspicious moment**  
One night she comes home later than planned. Her makeup is slightly messed up. She says the shoot ran long and she was tired. Player can confront or let it go.

**Tone of Act 2**  
Still mostly “normal.” The player feels the first real unease. Jessy is not cheating yet, but the foundation is being laid.

---

## Act 3 – Crossing the Line (Chapters 11–18)

This is where the NTR properly begins.

**The lingerie / swimwear jobs**  
Leon offers her higher-paying work that requires more skin. Jessy is reluctant but the money is significantly better. She asks for your opinion. Whatever you say, she eventually accepts.

**First sexual contact**  
During a private “test shoot,” Leon tells her she looks tense on camera. He offers to help her relax. It starts with massage, then touching, then oral, then sex.  
Jessy freezes at first, then goes along with it. Afterward she is quiet and guilty.

**Her return home**  
She comes back that night different. Quieter. She avoids sex with you. If the player pushes, she gives a weak excuse.  
Internal monologue (or later confession) shows she is telling herself: “It was just once. We needed the money.”

**Progression**

- More private sessions.
- Leon starts paying her extra “bonuses.”
- She begins to lie more smoothly.
- She starts comparing (quietly at first) — Leon is more confident, lasts longer, makes her feel desired in a way you haven’t in a long time.
- Player can find evidence: marks on her body, strange charges, messages, or walk in on a call.

**Key scene – First time she enjoys it**  
During one session she stops being passive and starts responding. Afterward she feels both pleasure and self-disgust. This is an important turning point.

**Tone of Act 3**  
Guilt is still strong. Jessy is not a slut yet. She is rationalizing hard. The player feels the relationship slipping.

---

## Act 4 – The New Normal (Chapters 19–26)

Jessy’s attitude shifts.

**Changes at home**

- She becomes less affectionate with you.
- She starts dressing nicer when she leaves for “work.”
- Sex with you becomes rare or one-sided.
- She sometimes comes home smelling of cologne or sex and doesn’t bother hiding it well.

**Open secrets**

- Leon begins dropping her off or picking her up.
- She stays overnight at the studio “for early morning shoots.”
- She starts talking about the money more openly while avoiding details of what she does for it.

**Possible mid-game confrontation**  
Player can confront her. Possible reactions depending on corruption level:

- She cries and promises to stop (but doesn’t).
- She gets defensive: “I’m doing this for us.”
- She admits part of it but downplays the sex.
- She gets cold and says you should be grateful for the money.

**Further escalation**

- Leon starts coming to the apartment.
- Jessy begins doing more explicit content (still under the label of modeling).
- She starts initiating the sexual side of the jobs herself.

**Tone of Act 4**  
The guilt is fading. Pleasure, money, and attention are winning. The relationship is clearly broken even if they still live together.

---

## Act 5 – Endings (Final Chapters)

**Ending A – Full NTR (Leave)**  
Jessy decides she wants the new life. She packs her things and moves in with Leon (or into a place he provides). Final scenes show her happy in her new role while you are left with the empty apartment and the money she used to bring in.

**Ending B – Kept Cuck**  
Jessy stays with you but the arrangement becomes open. She continues sleeping with Leon (and possibly others). You live off the money. She still calls you her boyfriend, but the sexual relationship is mostly gone or one-sided. Can include scenes where she makes you listen or watch.

**Ending C – Broken Return**  
Something goes too far (public exposure, pregnancy scare, Leon losing interest, etc.). Jessy quits modeling and comes back to you. The relationship continues, but trust and sexual chemistry are permanently damaged. Bittersweet and heavy.

**Ending D – Black / Complete Corruption**  
Jessy fully embraces the lifestyle. She cuts you off, blocks you, and becomes a higher-tier model who regularly sleeps with clients and photographers. Final montage shows her successful and sexually free while you are erased from her life.

---

## Themes & Tone Notes

- Early game: sympathy for Jessy. She genuinely starts for the money.
- Mid game: slow corruption mixed with guilt.
- Late game: the guilt fades and the pleasure + status take over.
- The player should feel both responsible (for encouraging or not stopping her) and powerless.
